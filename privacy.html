Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21

Biblia is built on a single principle: your time in Scripture is yours. This policy explains what data Biblia handles and what it never does.

Data we collect

None on our servers. Biblia does not have servers. Everything you do in the app — what you read, what you highlight, what you bookmark, what you write in your journal, what you choose to memorize, your reading history — stays on your device.

No account, ever

Biblia does not require you to create an account, sign in, or share an email address. There is nothing to sign up for. We do not know who you are.

No cloud sync, no servers

Biblia does not sync your data anywhere — not to our servers, not to iCloud, not to a third party. Your highlights and journal entries live in a private database on this device and nowhere else. If you delete the app, that data goes with it.

No analytics, no third-party trackers

Biblia includes zero analytics SDKs, zero tracking pixels, zero advertising frameworks. We do not measure how often you open the app, what verses you read, or how long you stay on a screen. Even we don't know.

No internet required

All five translations and Strong's Concordance are bundled into the app on day one. Biblia does not make network requests during normal use. If your device is offline, the app behaves identically.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily verse notification, your device — not our servers — schedules it locally. Notifications are generated entirely on your device. We never send a push notification to you and would not be able to.

Audio playback

Biblia uses your device's built-in text-to-speech to read Scripture aloud. The text never leaves your device. Apple's TTS engine processes the text on-device.

Apple Watch and widgets

The Apple Watch app and the Home and Lock Screen widgets both run entirely on your device and show only the daily verse from the bundled rotation. No data is exchanged between your iPhone, your Watch, and our (nonexistent) servers.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no SDKs that share data with third parties.
  • No advertising, anywhere, ever.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing your data.
  • No cloud sync.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

Biblia is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
