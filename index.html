The Quiet Bible App

Scripture, beautifully read.

Five translations. Strong's Concordance. On your device. No account, no feed, no ads.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch · iOS 18 and later · Free

Five translations, all offline

King James (1611), American Standard Version (1901), World English Bible, Berean Standard Bible, and Lexham English Bible. Switch with a tap. No download required, no internet needed.

Strong's Concordance, free

Tap any word in the King James to see its original Greek or Hebrew, transliteration, and definition — the same scholarly resource you'd find behind a paywall elsewhere.

Listen anywhere

Hear Scripture read aloud while you drive, walk, or rest. Background audio, Lock Screen controls, CarPlay-ready. Works for every translation.

Compare translations side-by-side

Open any chapter in two translations at once. Tap a verse on one side to line up the other.

Highlights, bookmarks, journal

Mark verses in five colors, save notes, write journal entries anchored to specific passages. Everything stays on your device.

Reading plans + verse memorization

Three reading plans included (Gospels in 30 days, Psalms & Proverbs in 90, New Testament in 90). Mark verses to memorize with spaced repetition.

Built differently on purpose.

Biblia has no account, no social feed, no daily streaks to lose, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The whole Bible lives on your phone. It will not try to retain your attention. It will not sell or share what you read. It will not ask you to sign in. It is, deliberately, just a reader.
