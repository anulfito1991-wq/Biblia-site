The Bible Reader Where Your Faith Grows

Read scripture. Watch faith bloom.

Six translations. Strong's Concordance. Fruits of the Spirit plans. Your own Bloom Garden. On your device. No account, no feed, no ads.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch · iOS 18 and later · Free

Six translations, all offline

King James (1611), American Standard Version (1901), World English Bible, Berean Standard Bible, Lexham English Bible, and Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish). Switch with a tap. No download required, no internet needed. Spanish-language devices open to Reina-Valera by default.

Fruits of the Spirit — 9 reading plans

Anchored in Galatians 5:22-23. Nine themed 7-day plans, one per fruit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Complete one and a fruit blooms in your Bloom Garden.

Bloom Garden

A quiet visual record of everything you've grown — sprouts for chapters read, leaves for verses memorized, flowers for plans completed, named fruits from Galatians 5, sunbursts for answered prayers. It only grows. Nothing ever shrinks. No streaks, no shame.

Strong's Concordance, free

Tap any word in the King James to see its original Greek or Hebrew, transliteration, and definition — the same scholarly resource you'd find behind a paywall elsewhere.

Listen anywhere

Hear Scripture read aloud while you drive, walk, or rest. Background audio, Lock Screen controls, CarPlay-ready. Works for every translation.

Compare translations side-by-side

Open any chapter in two translations at once. Tap a verse on one side to line up the other.

Prayer Garden

A categorized prayer journal — gratitude, requests, intercession, confession. Mark a prayer answered and a sunburst joins your Bloom Garden.

Daily Bloom + Seasonal Anchors

One verse, one contemplative prompt — sit with Scripture instead of skimming. A seasonal anchor verse surfaces for Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, and Pentecost.

Highlights, bookmarks, memorization

Mark verses in five colors, save bookmarks, memorize verses with gentle spaced repetition. Everything stays on your device.

Built differently on purpose.

FaithBloom has no account, no social feed, no daily streaks to lose, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The whole Bible lives on your phone. It will not try to retain your attention. It will not sell or share what you read. It will not ask you to sign in. It is, deliberately, a Bible reader anchored in Galatians 5 — your faith grows quietly, on your own terms.
